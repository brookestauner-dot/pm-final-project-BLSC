# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** A fleet dispatcher (mid-size 3PL, also represented on night shift) responsible for keeping driver routes current as conditions change.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Reassign routes and trust that drivers are acting on the current plan, not a stale one.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** The dashboard doesn't update status in real time and reassignments take 8-15 minutes to reach the driver app with no push notifications.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** WhatsApp, paper routes, screenshots
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Reassigns the route in RouteLogic (the system of record) — but cannot trust it to reach the driver in a usable window.
Immediately posts the same route change in a WhatsApp group, manually re-typing or describing the update, because this is the channel drivers actually check.
Watches the dispatcher dashboard for status updates, but treats it as unreliable — night-shift dispatchers report it shows stops as "in progress" up to an hour after they were actually completed.
Cross-checks or confirms delivery status by asking in WhatsApp or waiting for a driver's manual reply, rather than trusting the dashboard's live state.
In cases where a driver acted on a stale route before the WhatsApp message landed, initiates a phone call or manual correction to get them back on track.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** When the app fails to update the drivers in a usable window, you know they are not going to get the reroute message in time, so you automatically also update them on WhatsApp.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** "We keep a WhatsApp group as the real system"
